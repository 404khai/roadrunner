## Route corpus

`route-corpus.json` addresses endpoints by stable OSM node identity and covers
short, medium, long, same-origin, cross-component, directional, parallel-road,
and surface-policy cases. The pipeline test resolves those identities through
validated graph provenance and compares fresh and loaded graph routing.
